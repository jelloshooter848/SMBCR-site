The plan for **Super Mario Bros. Crossover: REMIX** has changed, and version 0.5.0 now means something new.

## Version 0.5.0: Classic mode

The first full release is a **true remake of Super Mario Bros. Crossover 3.1.21**. Very little changes apart from our
own original sprites and music. It has:

- the original's 10 heroes, plus Random;
- skins as in the original, each bringing its own moves;
- Super Mario Bros., The Lost Levels (played straight through, 1-1 to D-4) and the Special maps;
- Easy, Normal and Hard maps;
- the original's options: More settings, Weapons, Cheats, Survival and per-hero music;
- an **Expansion heroes** option, off by default, that adds Vic Viper, the Warriors of Light, Arthur and Yoshi.

## Until then: test builds

Test releases (v0.4.65, v0.4.66 and on) come out as the 0.5.0 work lands, and 0.5.0 follows a final check against the
original. You can [play the latest](../../play/) any time; please [report anything odd](../../community/#bugs). From now
on the release zips are called `smbc-remix-vX.Y.Z.zip` and `smbc-remix-vX.Y.Z-offline.zip`.

## After version 0.5.0

- **0.5.x:** about 60 more of the original's cosmetic looks.
- **0.6 to 0.9:** new heroes, through the Expansion heroes option.
- **1.0:** the story campaign, Chapter 1. It isn't in 0.5.0, and campaign saves you already have are kept.

The game stays where it is, at [jelloshooter848.github.io/SMBC](https://jelloshooter848.github.io/SMBC/).
