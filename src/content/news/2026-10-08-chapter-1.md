**Super Mario Bros. Crossover: REMIX** reaches its first big milestone: **Chapter 1, The Mushroom Kingdom**, version
0.5.0. It's free, it runs in your browser, and you can [play it right now](../../play/).

## What's in Chapter 1

- **All eight worlds of Super Mario Bros.**, from Mario's warm-up stage 1-0 to Bowser's castle at 8-4.
- **Nine heroes.** Mario starts alone. Luigi, Link, Mega Man, Samus, Simon, Ryu, Bill and the tank Sophia III are out
  there somewhere, brainwashed by Bowser's stolen star wand.
- **A themed world for every hero.** World 2 is Hyrule, World 3 Mega City, World 4 Planet Zebes, World 5
  Transylvania, World 6 Dragon Valley, World 7 Galuga Island and World 8 Bowser's Underworld.
- **Mini games.** Each hero is freed by a short round in the style of their own game: race Luigi to the flag, escape
  Zebes before the bomb goes off, duel the Masked Ninja on a moonlit roof.
- **The world map**, three save files, SMB3-style items and bonus games, and an optional training room for each hero.
- **The level editor**, with share links that carry your whole level in the URL.
- **Accessibility options:** remappable keys, gamepad and touch controls, reduced flashing, colour-blind palettes and
  a screen-reader announcer.

The Lost Levels are in the game too and playable in classic mode. Their story, the Lost Kingdom, is Chapter 2.
