**Super Mario Bros. Crossover: REMIX 0.5.0**, the first full release, is out. It is a true remake of Jay Pavlina's
_Super Mario Bros. Crossover_ 3.1.21: play Super Mario Bros. as the heroes of other NES classics, with all-new pixel art
and chiptune music. [Play it now](https://jelloshooter848.github.io/SMBC/), free, in any browser.

## What's in it

- **The original ten heroes**, plus Random: Mario, Luigi, Link, Samus, Simon, Mega Man, Bass, Bill, Ryu and Sophia III,
  each with the moves of their own game, and **22 alternate looks**. [Meet the heroes](../../heroes/) and watch each
  one play.
- **Three map sets:** Super Mario Bros., The Lost Levels (straight through, 1-1 to D-4) and SMB Special, each on
  **Easy, Normal or Hard**. See [Maps & modes](../../modes/).
- **Three save files per map set**, each with its own difficulty and rules: game mode (Survival too), weapons, More
  settings and which heroes are on.
- **Beat a set** and its file stays cleared, so you can replay any level of it. Beat it on Hard for that file's
  **Advanced cheats**. The original's cheats unlock as you play.
- **Music Lab:** write your own songs and play them on the game's synth (Options on the title screen).
- **Keyboard, gamepad or touch**, with remappable controls, reduced flashing, colour-blind palettes and screen-reader
  announcements. [How to play](../../how-to-play/).
- **Keep your saves** with a backup file, or back up online with a 12-character code and no account.
  [How it works](../../how-to-play/#saves).

On a phone, open the game with **Play**, then use **Add to Home Screen**: it opens like an app, full screen.
